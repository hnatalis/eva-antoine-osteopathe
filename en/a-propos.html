From the Cadre Noir in Saumur to the veterinary clinic.

2015

The turning point

Placement at the veterinary clinic of the Cadre Noir in Saumur. Animals and their wellbeing had always been my passion; this is where I discovered animal osteopathy.

2019 – 2024

Five years of training

C-NESOA in Châtel-Guyon. Alongside my studies, many placements in the equine world: a multi-discipline yard in Germany (Reitanlage Meir), a trotting yard (Écurie Charly-Mary), and time spent with vets, equine dentists, farriers, hoof-care specialists and breeders. Enough to build a whole-horse view of biomechanical demands.

2024

Graduation, then Italy

Once qualified, I spent three months near Rome practising alongside an experienced animal osteopath, with horses and dogs.

Then

Roland-Garros veterinary clinic

Back in France, I passed the examinations of the French veterinary board, then spent a year at the clinic in Boulogne-Billancourt: osteopathy consultations, and work with the physiotherapy team on small-animal rehabilitation.

Today

Working with clinics

I work with the Vet’Alouettes veterinary clinic in Les Herbiers and the Joli Mai clinic in Meudon-la-Forêt, with the aim of setting animal osteopathy within consistent, whole-body medical care.

Osteopathy belongs within medical care, not alongside it.

That is what five years of study and a year in a clinic taught me: the best results come when the vet, the osteopath and, for horses, the farrier and the equine dentist are all looking at the same animal.
