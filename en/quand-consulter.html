How do you know whether to call me?

An animal will not tell you it hurts, but its body will. Here are a few signs you can look out for that deserve closer attention, and most likely a consultation.

Because one small discomfort can change a whole movement, every compensation is worth observing.

Horse Dog Cat Cow Sheep · Goats Exotic pets

  • Difficulties in work: gait, rein, flying change, jumping
  • Lameness and irregularity
  • Loss of range of motion
  • Resistance or restlessness when groomed, saddled or mounted
  • Recurrent abscess and/or repeatedly losing the shoe on the same limb
  • Chronic metabolic disorders
  • Before and after foaling
  • Reproductive problems
  • Foal that does not suckle or does not get up
  • Deviation in limb conformation

Does one of these signs ring a bell? Call me

  • Avoids steps or kerbs
  • Drop in performance (working or sporting dog)
  • Growth problems
